Prototype notes

For the approval conversation: what this is and is not

What is real in this prototype

  • The full scoring model: eight indicators, adjustable weights, live recalculation
  • Every screen and interaction leadership would use: grid, drill-down, trends, maturity, targets, filters
  • Targets and department data are editable in the browser and export to Excel
  • The exported Excel file doubles as the template: fill it in, load it back, and the dashboard rebuilds

What is placeholder

  • All figures are illustrative sample data, not measured RAK Properties results
  • Department list, headcounts, tools and use cases are examples for demonstration
  • No live connection yet to tool admin consoles, the LMS or HR records
  • No login or role-based access. The production build would add both

Decisions needed to proceed

  • Confirm the eight indicators and their weights
  • Confirm the department list and who owns each data source
  • Agree the org-wide target and the minimum acceptable rate
  • Agree that reporting stays at department level, never individual
  • Approve the visual direction so the baseline collection can begin
